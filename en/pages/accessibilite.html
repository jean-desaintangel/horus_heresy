    Accessibility statement

    Drawn up in accordance with article 47 of French law no. 2005-102 of 11 February 2005 and decree no. 2019-768 of 24 July 2019.

    Jean de SAINT ANGEL is committed to making this site accessible to people with disabilities.

    Compliance status

    The site is partially compliant with the French General Accessibility Improvement Framework (RGAA) 4.1, owing to the non-compliances listed below.

    Test results

    The self-assessment carried out on 27/07/2026 shows that 93.5 % of applicable RGAA criteria are met.

    Scope and limits of this figure. It results from an analysis of the source code, contrast measurements calculated on the CSS variables and automated tests. It is based neither on an audit by a third-party body nor on tests carried out with people with disabilities. In particular, two checks remain to be carried out manually: the contrast of text overlaid on the gradients of the eighteen Legion themes (with Colour Contrast Analyser), and the actual rendering of the pages by a screen reader (NVDA, VoiceOver).

    Non-accessible content

    Non-compliances

    • Changes of language (criterion 8.7): some English game terms deliberately left untranslated (Jump Pack, Breacher) are not marked with a lang attribute. Planned fix: [date].

    Feedback and contact

    If you are unable to access any content or service, you can report it so that you can be directed to an accessible alternative:

    • via the contact form, choosing the "Technical problem" category;
    • stating the page concerned and the difficulty encountered, which makes fixing it much faster.

    Means of redress

    If you notice an accessibility defect preventing you from accessing content or a feature of the site, you report it to us and you do not receive a response, you are entitled to send your complaint or a referral request to the Défenseur des droits (French Rights Defender).

    Several options are available to you:

    • write a message to the Défenseur des droits (in French) (new window);
    • contact the Défenseur des droits delegate for your region (in French) (new window);
    • send a letter by post, free of charge and without a stamp:
