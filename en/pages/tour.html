    Turn sequence

    The missions in the rulebook are played over 4 rounds, in which each player takes a turn, one after the other.

    Effects Sub-phase — resolve everything that triggers "at the start of the turn": special rules, powers, lasting effects.

    • Reserves Sub-phase — units kept off the table (Reserves, Deep Strike…) attempt to arrive in play.
    • Move Sub-phase — you move your units: a normal move, or "Rush" to go faster (at the cost of shooting and charging).
    • Rout Sub-phase — Routed units Fall Back towards your deployment zone (see statuses).

    Movement Phase in detail →

    • Attack Sub-phase — units shoot, one at a time, following the resolution sequence detailed below.
    • Morale Sub-phase — units that have suffered heavily test their morale; if they fail, hello Tactical Statuses.

    Shooting Phase in detail →

    • Charge Sub-phase — units declare their charges and hurl themselves into contact (the opponent may react: Overwatch, Evade…).
    • Challenge Sub-phase — champions call each other out to a duel (see the Challenge page).
    • Fight Sub-phase — everyone strikes, in descending order of Initiative.
    • Resolution Sub-phase — count up each side's points: the loser tests its morale and risks being routed.

    Assault Phase in detail →

    • Effects Sub-phase — resolve "end of turn" effects.
    • Statuses Sub-phase — some Tactical Statuses expire or change.
    • Victory Sub-phase — check the objectives and score Victory Points.
